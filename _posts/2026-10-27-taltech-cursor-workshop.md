Last week, I got an unique experience to co-host a Cursor Lab at my university TalTech with the local Tallinn Cursor (now SpaceXSI) Community ambassador Manuel Zimmermann. 

Presented a new MCP and skill guide I wrote for this event with the intention to introduce agentic engineering practices to TalTech’s IT students or to beginner developers in-general.

With the advent of LLMs, using agents is a valuable skill to learn beside the usual legacy CS curriculum.
